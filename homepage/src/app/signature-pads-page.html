Think of the signature as a timeline. Each time the pen touches the surface, a new stroke begins. Its points stay in drawing order. t counts milliseconds from the first contact, including pauses between strokes. For example, points at 0 and 45 ms were drawn 45 ms apart; if the next stroke starts at 260 ms, the pause is preserved too. x and y locate the pen on the recorded canvas; pressure ranges from 0 to 1.

This short excerpt shows how to read the data. The bound value retains every point, so you can replay the movement. The SVG stores the finished lines as a standalone image, without their timing; svgChange emits it after each stroke and after Clear.

Bind a reactive form control instead of [(value)] when the pad belongs to a form. Set its value to a saved GoodSignatureData object to show the signature again. Clear sets it to null.
