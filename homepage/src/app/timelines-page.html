Set the shared choices on <good-timeline>. Give only exceptional steps their own overrides. These are the accepted values:

Form appearance accepts simple (plain line), detailed (text panels), or illustrated (graphics above the line). Default: simple. Direction and size orientation accepts auto, horizontal, or vertical. Auto turns vertical below 680 px of component width. size accepts small, default, or large. Defaults: auto and default. Palette tone accepts brand (default), secondary, or mono. color and optional completedColor accept brand, accent, success, warning, info, destructive, neutral, or violet. Mono removes all color. Progress [currentIndex] starts at 0; earlier steps are completed. Set it to the step count when all are done. A step's state may be completed, current, upcoming, error, or deactivated. Boolean deactivated works on a step or the whole timeline. Markers and graphics marker accepts number (default), icon, or dot; a step may override it. A step may use iconName, custom [icon], or imageSrc with imageAlt. The image wins. In illustrated mode, endpoint="start" and endpoint="end" put first and last icons on the line. Step content Each step needs unique id and visible title. Optional eyebrow, description, and transitionLabel add context; a transition labels the line leaving that step. ariaLabel names the timeline for assistive technology.

Built-in iconName values: check, clipboard-list, file-text, house, map-pin, package-check, shield-check, and truck. A step's color wins over completedColor; tone="mono" wins over both. Use [steps] instead of child tags when the step count comes from data.

Write one of these strings in color="..." on <good-timeline>. The swatches use the active light or dark theme. accent is a good default for an illustrated process; brand is the component default.

  • accentTheme-aware blue · recommended process accent
  • brandCore brand blue · default; same as accent in light mode
  • successGreen · successful outcome
  • warningContrast amber or yellow · needs attention
  • infoSky blue · informational state
  • destructiveRed · error or destructive outcome
  • neutralGray · quiet process information
  • violetPurple · optional category accent

Finished steps already stand out without a second hue. Set completedColor="success" only when green has a specific meaning for completion. A step's own color overrides both parent colors. tone="secondary" makes ordinary steps neutral; tone="mono" removes all color.

tone changes the palette without changing the layout. brand uses the GoodUI accent, secondary uses a quieter neutral fill, and mono gives the entire timeline a black, white, and gray print treatment. The mono tone also removes color from step overrides and images.
