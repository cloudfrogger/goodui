Data visualizations World maps Explore country-level data on a zoomable, draggable map. Use the same typed sources as the other GoodUI charts. A row identifies a country with its two-letter ISO code. An optional date or datetime key turns repeated rows into a time series. The map loads its geometry locally; your data stays in your application. The same source supplies percentage details for 18 countries. Choose World above to see nearby details combine into their mean; zoom in to separate them again. The small number on a combined detail tells you how many countries it represents. Bars and pie overlays Use two or more numeric keys for a small bar chart or pie in each country. The first value uses the first marker color, the second uses the next; [colors] replaces the palette. When nearby countries would crowd the map, GoodUI averages each measure and shows one bar or pie with a country count.
