The right-hand topbar area is a container named good-main-menu. Put the actions you need inside it, in the order you want. Omit an action to remove it. The container owns alignment and responsive gaps, while each action owns its trigger and behavior. Try it Compose your topbarUse the same container as a direct child of good-topbar for a standalone topbar. When you supply children, only those children render. With no projected content, MainMenu keeps its standard actions and existing inputs. Configure projected children directly.
