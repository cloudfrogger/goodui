Select text before applying an inline format. The font menu previews locally available families; use Format for headings and blocks. The table grid inserts rows and columns. Tap a cell for its nearby actions and resize grips. Drag the right grip to resize its column or the bottom grip to resize its row. Use Shift-click, Ctrl/Command-click, or the menu's touch-friendly selection mode to choose several cells before changing them all to header or body cells.
