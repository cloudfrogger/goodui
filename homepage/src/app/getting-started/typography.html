Text columns

Choose two, three, or four columns. GoodUI stacks them as space narrows and keeps each column's headings and paragraphs in the usual text rhythm. Visibility follows the width of this container.

Two columns (default) PlanWrite the first part of your content here. BuildWrite the second part here. Both columns stack on narrow screens. Three columns DiscoverFind the need.
